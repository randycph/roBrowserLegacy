Game Changelog

November 17, 2026
  • Increased regular and boss drop rates from 75x to 300x.
  • Increased MVP drops from 75x to 200x and MVP card rates from 50x to 200x.
  • Increased regular, boss, and MVP item drop rates from 20x to 50x. Experience rates remain 200x.
  • Increased MVP card drop rates from 15x to 25x.
  • Increased regular, boss, and MVP item drop rates from 50x to 75x.
  • Increased MVP card drop rates from 25x to 50x.
  • Fixed Claim All so it also collects attachments from NPC and system mail.
  • Use Navigation to search for a monster and select Move to see a route to its spawn location.
November 16, 2026
  • Added Healer NPCs that restore HP and SP and provide Level 10 Blessing and Increase Agility for 30 minutes.
  • First Aid Box (5) now includes zeny, Butterfly Wings, and Fly Wings.
  • Added Warper NPCs in towns, including respawn points.
  • Added buy and sell NPCs to the NPC list so players can trade without leaving their spot.
  • Fixed an issue that prevented movement while a shop was open.
  • Players can now move by clicking a destination on the minimap.
  • Improved mobile controls when playing on a mobile device.
